Process

How a project actually runs

Week zero — the hard conversation

We scope in writing: what's in, what's out, what it costs, and what would make us tell you to stop. Fixed price, fixed scope, one page. If the fit isn't right we say so and point you elsewhere — we keep a list of studios we trust.

Weeks 1–2 — design in the open

Wireframes become a clickable prototype in days. You're in the file, not in a status meeting. We converge on structure first and polish second, because polish on the wrong structure is waste.

Weeks 3–8 — build in public

Every Thursday: a staging link with what changed, what broke, and what we learned. No surprises in week eight, because there were no surprises in week three.

Launch + 30 — handover that holds

Documentation, a walkthrough recorded for your future hires, and 30 days of on-call while the product meets reality. Then we get out of the way — or stay on a light retainer if you want us around.
