Features

Everything you ask a data scientist, minus the queue

Meridian is built for product managers who need answers today, with enough rigor that your data team will actually trust the output.

Ask in plain English

“Which plan converts best from trial?” gets you the answer with the query behind it — inspectable, reusable, and shareable with one link.

Anomaly explanations

When a metric moves, Meridian decomposes it by segment, platform, and cohort and tells you which slice is responsible — automatically.

Alerts with context

Slack alerts that arrive with the why attached: the affected segment, the likely cause, and the two follow-up questions worth asking.

Warehouse-native

Runs on Snowflake, BigQuery, or Postgres. Your data never leaves your warehouse — Meridian queries it, caches the aggregates, nothing else.

Metric governance

One definition of “active user”, versioned and enforced. When marketing and finance quote different numbers, Meridian settles it.

Board-ready reports

One click turns any answer into a formatted monthly summary — trends, movers, and open questions — that your CEO will actually read.

How it works

Live in an afternoon

No SQL to write, no events to relabel, no implementation project.

Connect your source

Point Meridian at your warehouse, Segment, or PostHog. It reads your schema and proposes a metric layer you approve — usually under an hour, including review.

Ask your first question

Type like you'd Slack a colleague. Meridian writes the query, shows its work, and saves every answer as a reusable, versioned metric.

Put it on autopilot

Subscribe metrics to Slack or email. Meridian watches for movement, explains anomalies, and nudges you only when something deserves attention.
